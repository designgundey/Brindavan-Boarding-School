beginning.

Education is about who a child becomes, as much as what they learn.

Situated at Seeyok Fatak in Mirik, Brindaban Boarding School is part of the Darjeeling hills’ educational community. Public school listings describe a co-educational boarding school with an ICSE and ISC academic pathway.

For families exploring education in the hills, the school brings together classroom learning and the experience of growing within a school community.
