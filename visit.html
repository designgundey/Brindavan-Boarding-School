GETTING HERE

Reaching
Mirik &
Seeyok Fatak.

Mirik is in the Darjeeling hills of West Bengal, reachable from the region’s main gateway towns.

01NEAREST AIRPORT

Bagdogra Airport

The nearest major airport, with onward road travel of roughly 2–3 hours to Mirik depending on traffic and route.

02NEAREST RAILWAY

New Jalpaiguri (NJP)

The main railhead for the region, similarly connected to Mirik by road.

03BY ROAD

From Darjeeling or Siliguri

Mirik is connected by hill roads to both Darjeeling town and Siliguri — confirm current travel times and routes before your visit.

Travel times vary with road and weather conditions in the hills. Confirm the best route with the school ahead of an admission visit.
