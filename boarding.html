Choosing a boarding school is a personal decision. This page walks through what boarding life at Brindaban generally involves, so a visit can focus on the specific details that matter to your family.

WHAT TO EXPLORE ON YOUR VISIT

01

Everyday life

Talk through accommodation, meals, daily routines and how new boarders settle in during their first weeks.

02

Care & communication

Meet the boarding team and ask about supervision, medical support, and how families stay in touch with their child.

03

Study & independence

Discuss prep/study time, personal responsibility for belongings and routines, and the balance between structure and downtime.

WHAT TO ASK ABOUT

Questions worth
raising on a
visit.

Every family’s priorities differ — these are common starting points.

01DAILY LIFE

Meals & routine

Ask about meal arrangements, dietary accommodations, wake and lights-out times, and how weekends and holidays are structured.

02WELLBEING

Health & safety

Ask about on-site or nearby medical support, how illness is handled, and the supervision ratio for younger boarders.

03SETTLING IN

New boarders

Ask how the school supports a child’s first term — orientation, buddy systems, and how homesickness is handled.

Boarding facilities, capacity, age requirements and charges should be confirmed directly with the school — they are not collected or guaranteed by this website.
