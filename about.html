Brindaban Boarding School sits at Seeyok Fatak in Mirik, part of the Darjeeling hills’ close-knit educational community. Public listings describe it as a co-educational boarding school following the ICSE and ISC pathway, and this page brings together what is publicly known about its story, setting and outlook.

OUR STORY

Rooted in the
hills of
Mirik.

A school shaped by its setting.

Mirik is a small hill town in the Darjeeling district of West Bengal, known for its tea gardens, orange orchards and Sumendu Lake. Brindaban Boarding School was established there to give children of the region, and boarding families from further afield, an education that pairs a structured academic pathway with the everyday rhythms of hill-town life.

The school’s crest carries the motto “Love All, Serve All” alongside a torch, an open book and the year 2005, reflecting the values the school asks its students to carry: knowledge, service and community.

WHAT WE VALUE

The ideas behind
a Brindaban education.

Three threads run through school life, from the classroom to the boarding house.

01CURIOSITY

A love of learning

Encouraging students to ask questions, explore ideas beyond the textbook and take ownership of how they learn.

02CHARACTER

Discipline & integrity

Boarding life asks for responsibility early: for one’s time, belongings, relationships and choices.

03COMMUNITY

Love all, serve all

The school motto, taken from the crest, frames community life: care for peers, staff and the wider Mirik community.
