A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Parents ask.
We understand.

Where is the school located?

The school is listed at Seeyok Fatak, Mirik, Darjeeling, West Bengal, India, PIN 734214. Visit the Visit page to plan your route and confirm the entrance before travelling.

Which board does the school follow?

Public listings identify ICSE and ISC, with CISCE school code WB353. Please confirm the current affiliation and class-specific availability directly with the school.

Is boarding available?

The school is listed as a co-educational boarding school. See the Boarding Life page and ask the school about current residential availability, age requirements, facilities and charges.

What are the fees and admission dates?

A reliable current fee schedule and admission calendar were not available in the public sources reviewed. Request the latest written fee breakdown and admission dates directly from the school.

What should we bring for an admission visit?

Ask the school for its official checklist first. It may be useful to have your child’s recent school reports and basic age and class details ready for the discussion.

Who do we contact to apply?

Reach the school through its official social page, or plan an in-person visit to Seeyok Fatak, Mirik to speak with the admissions team directly.
